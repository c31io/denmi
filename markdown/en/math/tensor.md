# Tensor Algebra\n\n## Definition\nA vector space $V\otimes W$ together with a bilinear map $\otimes: V\times W\to V\otimes W$ is called the **tensor product** of $V$ and $W$. It is defined by the universal property below.\n\n## Key Properties\n- **Bilinearity**: $(v+v',w)=v\otimes w+v'\otimes w$, $(v,w+w')=v\otimes w+v\otimes w'$.\n- **Basis**: If $\{e_i\}$ and $\{f_j\}$ are bases of $V$ and $W$, then $\{e_i\otimes f_j\}$ is a basis of $V\otimes W$.\n- **Universality**: For any bilinear $B:V\times W\to U$ there is a unique linear $\tilde B:V\otimes W\to U$ with $B(v,w)=\tilde B(v\otimes w)$.\n- **Symmetric/antisymmetric subspaces**: $S^2V=(V\otimes V)/\langle v\otimes w-w\otimes v\rangle$, $\Lambda^2V=(V\otimes V)/\langle v\otimes w+w\otimes v\rangle$.\n- **Metric tensor**: A non‑degenerate symmetric bilinear $g_{ij}$ on $V$ yields an isomorphism $V\cong V^*$.\n- **Riemann curvature**: $R^i_{\;jkl}=\partial_k\Gamma^i_{jl}-\partial_l\Gamma^i_{jk}+\Gamma^i_{km}\Gamma^m_{jl}-\Gamma^i_{lm}\Gamma^m_{jk}$.\n\n## Main Theorem (Universality of Tensor Product)\nFor any bilinear map $\phi:V\times W\to U$ there exists a unique linear map $\tilde\phi:V\otimes W\to U$ such that $\phi(v,w)=\tilde\phi(v\otimes w)$. This property characterises $V\otimes W$ up to unique isomorphism.
