# Functional Analysis

## Definition
Functional analysis studies infinite‑dimensional vector spaces (e.g., Banach and Hilbert spaces) and mappings between them, emphasizing topological and algebraic structure.

## Key Properties
- Banach spaces are complete normed vector spaces.
- Hilbert spaces are complete inner‑product spaces; they are Banach with the norm induced by the inner product.
- Bounded linear operators between Banach spaces form a Banach algebra.
- The dual space X* of a Banach space X is itself a Banach space.
- Riesz representation: for a Hilbert space H, every continuous linear functional is given by an inner product with a unique vector.

## Main Theorem
The Hahn‑Banach theorem extends linear functionals preserving sublinear bounds, yielding rich duality. For a bounded self‑adjoint operator $A$ on a Hilbert space, the spectral theorem expresses $A=\int_{\sigma(A)}\lambda\,dE_\lambda$ with a projection‑valued measure $E$, yielding a functional calculus. In particular, for a Hilbert space $H$, the Riesz representation theorem identifies $H$ with its continuous dual $H^*$ via the map $y\mapsto\langle\cdot,y\rangle$.
