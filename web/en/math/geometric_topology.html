Geometric Topology

Definition

The study of manifolds and their embeddings, focusing on topological properties preserved under homeomorphisms and diffeomorphisms. ## Key Properties - Low-dimensional topology (\(n \leq 4\)) - Classification of closed surfaces: \(S^2, T^2, \Sigma_g\) - Knot equivalence via ambient isotopy - 3-manifold invariants (e.g., fundamental group) ## Main Theorem Classification of Closed Surfaces. Any compact connected 2-manifold is homeomorphic to a connected sum of tori \(T^2\) and projective planes \(\mathbb{R}P^2\). Specifically, \(M \cong \#_g T^2 \#_k \mathbb{R}P^2\) where \(g \geq 0\), \(k \geq 0\).
