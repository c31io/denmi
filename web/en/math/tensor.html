Tensor Algebra## Definitionvector space \(V\otimes W\) together with a bilinear map \(\otimes: V\times W\to V\otimes W\) is called the tensor product of \(V\) and \(W\). It is defined by the universal property below.## Key Properties- Bilinearity: \((v+v',w)=v\otimes w+v'\otimes w\), \((v,w+w')=v\otimes w+v\otimes w'\).- Basis: If \(\{e_i\}\) and \(\{f_j\}\) are bases of \(V\) and \(W\), then \(\{e_i\otimes f_j\}\) is a basis of \(V\otimes W\).- Universality: For any bilinear \(B:V\times W\to U\) there is a unique linear \(\tilde B:V\otimes W\to U\) with \(B(v,w)=\tilde B(v\otimes w)\).- Symmetric/antisymmetric subspaces: \(S^2V=(V\otimes V)/\langle v\otimes w-w\otimes v\rangle\), \(\Lambda^2V=(V\otimes V)/\langle v\otimes w+w\otimes v\rangle\).- Metric tensor: A non‑degenerate symmetric bilinear \(g_{ij}\) on \(V\) yields an isomorphism \(V\cong V^*\).- Riemann curvature: \(R^i_{\;jkl}=\partial_k\Gamma^i_{jl}-\partial_l\Gamma^i_{jk}+\Gamma^i_{km}\Gamma^m_{jl}-\Gamma^i_{lm}\Gamma^m_{jk}\).## Main Theorem (Universality of Tensor Product)any bilinear map \(\phi:V\times W\to U\) there exists a unique linear map \(\tilde\phi:V\otimes W\to U\) such that \(\phi(v,w)=\tilde\phi(v\otimes w)\). This property characterises \(V\otimes W\) up to unique isomorphism.
